MISSION BRIEFING

The premise

An astronaut wakes in the medbay of FGRYYNE, a research vessel drifting through deep space, with no memory of how he got there. The crew is gone, the power is failing and the fuel is running low. To survive he has to keep the ship alive. To understand it, he has to answer one question: who were these people?

SYSTEMS

Key features

01

A mystery told by the ship

Uncover what happened through logs, objects and empty rooms.

02

Tractor-beam mining

Pull in asteroids to keep the ship fuelled and moving.

03

Ship hazards

Survive failures like a hull breach in total darkness.

04

Dread, not jump scares

Slow, suspenseful exploration: horror built on contrast.

05

Fully voiced

Every voice, from the astronaut to the ship's computer, performed by the developer.
